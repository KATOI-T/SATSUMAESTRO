- **Endorsement**: anything that suggests KATO IT, LLC made, sponsors or endorses your product or

## How to write the name

- Write **SATSUMAESTRO** (all capitals) or **Satsumaestro**. `satsumaestro` in lower case is the
  command and package name.
- Do not split or mix the name, for example "SATSUMA ESTRO", "Satsuma Estro" or "SATSUMAestro".
- The mark is not registered yet, so please do not use ® with it.
